Common questions

How do I sign up for DriveLink?
Get the app via our beta link (runs through Apple's TestFlight, App Store coming soon), open it and sign up for free with your email address.

Is DriveLink free?
Yes, using DriveLink is free.

I found a bug. What now?
Send a short description to support.drivelink.app@gmail.com, ideally with a screenshot, your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I delete my account?
Right in the app under Profile → Settings → "Delete account". Your account and all related data are removed immediately as described in our privacy policy. If anything goes wrong, reach us at support.drivelink.app@gmail.com.
