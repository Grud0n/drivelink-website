Step by step

  1. Create a group. Say, "Sunday Run". You invite friends, then every member can add more friends. The group has its own chat.
  2. Plan or share a route. Plan a tour with stops or as a loop, or use a road you've already driven and recorded. It shows up in the chat as a map everyone can open.
  3. Vote. Put two or more routes up for a vote. The group votes, and the route with the most votes gets marked.
  4. Start the convoy. Start a convoy straight from the winning route. The group gets notified and can join with one tap.
  5. Drive together. Everyone is live on the map. The destination can be a route, an event or any point on the map.

Navigator & chat

One person leads as navigator, and the role can be handed over. Use the convoy chat to sort out breaks and fuel stops.

Ends by itself

A convoy ends automatically after 12 hours at the latest. Nobody stays visible for days by accident.

Your privacy

You decide who sees you on the map: everyone, friends only or nobody. With "Everyone" and "Friends only", your convoy sees you too while you're driving together. A Ghost Zone around your home keeps you invisible there to everyone, including your convoy. No drive gives away where you live.
