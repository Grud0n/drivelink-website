Why meets are so hard to find

Most meets live in Instagram stories, Facebook events or WhatsApp groups. If you're not in the right group, you miss them, and a story is gone after 24 hours. In DriveLink the meet stays put, with address, time and map.

How to find meets with DriveLink

  1. Open Events. The Events tab shows every upcoming meet around you, as a list or on the map.
  2. Filter and sort. Filter by type (Car Meet, Track Day, Drive, Spontaneous) and sort by date or distance. Search finds meets by name, place or organiser.
  3. RSVP. One tap and you're in. For meets with limited spots you can see how many are left.
  4. Get there. Start navigation to the meeting point right from the event, or roll in together with friends as a convoy.

What kind of meets you'll find

Car meets & tuner meets

Regular meets in car parks and on private grounds, from the local crew to the big scene event.

Classic, electric, motorbike

Organisers set which vehicles a meet is for, for example stock, tuned, classic, electric or motorbike.

Drives & track days

Group drives with a meeting point and dates at the track, in the same overview as the meets.

Spontaneous meets

"Happening now near you" shows meets that are coming together at short notice.

Organising a meet yourself?

Creating an event in DriveLink takes a few minutes: title, date, meeting point, cover image, up to three photos and, if you like, a site map showing entry, exit and parking. Set recurring meets to weekly or monthly once. A meet can be public or private, and you can cap the number of spots.

You always see who's coming and don't have to repost the details in every group.

Is my area covered yet?

DriveLink is new and started in Germany. In many regions there aren't many meets listed yet. The app works anywhere, though, and the more organisers add their dates, the fuller the map gets. Know a meet that's missing? Add it or tell the organiser.
