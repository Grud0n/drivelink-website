DriveLink FAQ

DriveLink is a free iPhone app for car meets and group drives. Here are the most common questions, answered briefly.

Car meets & drives

Which app is good for car meets?

DriveLink is a free iPhone app made for car meets and group drives. You see car meets, tuner and classic car meets, drives and track days on a map, can sort by date or distance, RSVP and start navigation to the meeting point. Organisers add their meets themselves, including recurring ones with a site map.

More: finding car meets with DriveLink

How do I find car meets near me?

Open the Events tab in DriveLink and sort by distance or switch to the map view. You can filter by type (Car Meet, Track Day, Drive, Spontaneous) and search by place or organiser. Short-notice meets show up under "Happening now near you".

Which app is good for group drives?

DriveLink lets you plan group drives in a group: plan or share a route, vote between several routes and start the winning route straight away as a convoy. On the road, all drivers see each other live on one map, and the app shows when someone falls behind.

More: group drives with DriveLink

What is a convoy in DriveLink?

A convoy is a drive with several cars together. All drivers appear live on the map, one person leads as navigator, there is a convoy chat and a shared destination. A convoy ends automatically after 12 hours at the latest.

How do I add my car meet to DriveLink?

Get the app, create a free account and add a new event under Events with title, date, meeting point and type. Optionally add a cover image, photos and a site map, and set the meet to repeat weekly or monthly.

In which countries does DriveLink work?

DriveLink works anywhere and is available in English and German. It started in Germany, so that is where most meets are listed so far. Elsewhere the map fills up as drivers and organisers add their meets.

App & account

Is DriveLink free?

Yes, DriveLink is free to download and use. Finding meets, creating events, groups and convoys cost nothing.

Is DriveLink available on Android?

No, DriveLink is currently available for iPhone only.

How do I get DriveLink?

DriveLink is currently in open beta via Apple's TestFlight. Open the beta link, install the free TestFlight app and download DriveLink through it. DriveLink will be available directly on the App Store soon.

Can everyone see where I live?

No. You decide who sees you on the map: everyone, friends only or nobody. Until you choose, you are invisible. With a Ghost Zone around your home you are invisible there to everyone, including friends and your convoy.

Who is behind DriveLink?

DriveLink is developed by Jakob Lischke in Dresden, Germany, as an independent app made by car people for car people. Contact: Support.
